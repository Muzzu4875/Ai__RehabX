What is AI-RehabX?

AI-RehabX detects sports injuries in real time and builds a rehabilitation program around each athlete. It pairs modern technology with sports science so athletes recover faster, train smarter and perform better.

  • Smart detectionFinds injuries early and accurately.
  • Real-time monitoringTracks your movement and health.
  • Personalized rehabilitationPlans based on your needs.
  • Better performanceGet back stronger and safer.

Our solution

AI, wearable sensors and computer vision work together to monitor, analyze and guide rehabilitation as it happens.

  1. CameraMonitors movement
  2. Wearable sensorsHeart rate, motion, temperature
  3. AI analysisDetects posture, angles, risk
  4. Personalized feedbackGuidance and progress report

Key features

AI injury detection and risk prediction

Detects abnormal movement and predicts injury risk early.

Real-time posture and movement analysis

Tracks joint angles, balance and exercise form.

Vital signs monitoring

Monitors heart rate, body temperature and activity.

Personalized rehabilitation guidance

Customized exercises, safe load progression and recovery guidance.

Real-time progress tracking

Shows recovery progress with instant feedback and measurable metrics.

Return-to-play assessment

System flow: injury detection to full recovery

A continuous feedback loop sends results back into the plan after every session.

Hardware and software

Hardware: sensors, devices, capture

  • Camera system: motion capture, posture analysis, video tracking
  • Wearable sensors: heart rate, temperature, motion and activity
  • AI model on an edge device: ESP32 / microcontroller
  • Inertial measurement unit: joint angles, balance detection
  • Communication module: Wi-Fi / Bluetooth, cloud sync
  • Power supply: rechargeable, long life, portable

Software: analyze, guide, personalize

  • AI algorithm: injury detection, movement analysis, risk prediction
  • User dashboard (web / mobile): real-time data, alerts
  • Personalized rehab plan: exercises, intensity, recovery timeline
  • Data storage and cloud: secure, accessible anytime
  • AI feedback system: real-time suggestions
  • Analytics and reporting: long-term health tracking
